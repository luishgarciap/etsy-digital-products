## Promotion criteria

A candidate moves to pilot when we can answer:

1. Who buys it and why?
2. Is there enough demand?
3. What is weak or incomplete in current offers?
4. What will we create differently or better?
5. What price/bundle is plausible?
6. Can we produce it efficiently and legally?
7. Are the unit economics acceptable?
8. Can we create several coherent related products?
