Public result counts show **supply and visible market activity**, not true search demand.

## Preferred source: Etsy Marketplace Insights

Etsy Marketplace Insights provides direct Etsy search data:

- searches for a keyword in the last 30 days;
- number of Etsy listings;
- related terms and trend information.

Etsy currently permits **15 free keyword searches per week** and keeps results available for seven days. [[1]](../references.md#ref-1)

Official Etsy data should take priority when it conflicts with third-party estimates.
