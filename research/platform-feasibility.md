## Executive conclusion

There is no current platform-level blocker to using **Brazil as the operating base while targeting international Etsy buyers**. Brazil is eligible for Etsy Payments through Payoneer. [[27]](../references.md#ref-27) [[4]](../references.md#ref-4)

## Marketplace scale

As of 30 September 2026, Etsy reported approximately **87.5 million active buyers**, **5.836 million active sellers** and Q3 GMS of about **US$2.647 billion**. [[9]](../references.md#ref-9)

These figures establish marketplace scale, not profitability for a new shop.

## Brazil and payments

For Payoneer-based Etsy Payments:

- available Etsy funds are sent to Payoneer each Monday;
- withdrawal to a linked bank account typically takes 2–5 business days;
- Etsy deposits to Payoneer in USD. [[4]](../references.md#ref-4)

Detailed account arrangements can be resolved later; they are not part of product discovery.

## Digital delivery

Etsy supports instant-download and made-to-order digital products. Instant-download listings can currently contain **up to five files, maximum 20 MB each**. Digital items must be made and/or designed by the seller. [[6]](../references.md#ref-6)

This affects packaging and instructions.

## Canva

Canva permits selling original designs and templates under licence conditions. Templates must be original; Canva states that Pro content may be used in templates sold as Canva template links, but cannot simply be redistributed as standalone content. [[26]](../references.md#ref-26)

This must be checked product-by-product.

## AI-assisted creation

Etsy permits seller-prompted AI creations when the seller contributes creative input and discloses AI use. AI prompt bundles and entirely bot-generated content are restricted, and normal IP rules still apply. [[7]](../references.md#ref-7)

> **Need → product concept → original creative process → appropriate tools**

AI is a production capability, not the product strategy.

## Search and discovery

Etsy search considers query matching plus listing/shop quality. Etsy identifies titles, tags, attributes, descriptions, first photo, reviews and other signals as part of matching/ranking. [[8]](../references.md#ref-8)

Marketplace Insights provides direct 30-day keyword search/listing data and is the preferred source for demand validation. [[1]](../references.md#ref-1)

## Advertising

Etsy states that Ads work best when listings already receive buyer interest and are unlikely to turn a low-demand listing into a top seller. New shops currently wait 15 days before starting Etsy Ads. [[24]](../references.md#ref-24)

The first pilot should therefore prioritise product/search fit before paid promotion.

## Constraints to carry into selection

- Originality and IP
- Canva/content licensing
- AI disclosure and creative contribution
- Low-price fee sensitivity
- Digital file limits and instructions
- Search relevance/listing quality
- Payoneer/FX costs
- Tax/local legal obligations before formal operation
