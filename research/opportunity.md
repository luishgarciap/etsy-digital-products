## Executive view

We are not looking for a generic “low-competition niche”. We are looking for a **specific buyer + use case + product format** where the following align:

> **Demand · buyer intent · unmet need · viable economics · repeatable production**

## 1. Café / small-business resources

Potential products include café and bakery menus, price lists, loyalty materials, seasonal signs, social-media assets and coordinated business kits.

Initial third-party screening is encouraging for specific terms:

- `coffee menu`: ~18,100 estimated monthly searches, 4,368 competing listings, typical price around US$7. [[10]](../references.md#ref-10)
- `matcha menu`: ~4,400 searches, 191 listings, typical price around US$6.85. [[11]](../references.md#ref-11)
- `food menu template`: ~2,900 searches against 21,511 listings, typical price around US$4.99. [[12]](../references.md#ref-12)

The current hypothesis is therefore **specific café/bakery/matcha use case + coherent bundle**, not a generic restaurant-menu template.

## 2. Invitations / celebrations

The broad category is active but crowded:

- `birthday invitations`: ~90,500 estimated monthly searches and more than one million competing listings. [[13]](../references.md#ref-13)
- Etsy currently shows 5,000+ items for baby-shower invitation templates. [[15]](../references.md#ref-15)

The opportunity, if any, is likely to be narrower: occasion, culture, language, format or bundle.

## 3. Bilingual learning

`spanish flashcards` currently shows ~6,600 estimated monthly searches and 3,083 competing listings in the source's competition table, with a typical price around US$3.40. [[14]](../references.md#ref-14)

Etsy's public bilingual-flashcard market page also shows 1,000+ items with established review activity. [[16]](../references.md#ref-16)

Low unit prices make bundles and production efficiency important.

## 4. Catholic / faith-based products

This is a newer candidate. Etsy's `Catholic prayer cards` market page currently shows 5,000+ items, including prayer guides, children's prayer cards and printable resources with substantial review histories. [[19]](../references.md#ref-19)

That proves active supply and buyer activity, **not attractive demand-to-competition economics**. It now needs equivalent Marketplace Insights and review analysis.

Potential directions to test include children, First Communion, Confirmation, Rosary/prayer resources, bilingual materials and modern faith-based printables.

## 5. Colouring / creative products

This remains a product-creation hypothesis rather than a validated market direction. A specific buyer and use case should be identified before production begins.

AI may assist the creative workflow, but Etsy requires seller creative input and disclosure when AI is used. [[7]](../references.md#ref-7)

## Current conclusion

The strongest current evidence supports deeper investigation of:

1. specific café/gastronomy sub-niches;
2. Spanish-English / bilingual learning;
3. narrow invitation sub-niches rather than generic invitations.

Catholic and colouring products remain open until equivalent screening is completed.
