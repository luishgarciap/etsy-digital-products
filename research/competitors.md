## Purpose

Competitor research is used to understand what buyers already purchase, expected quality, packaging, price structure and unresolved customer friction. Public sales and review counts show activity; they do not reveal profit.

| Shop | Current public signal | What to study |
|---|---|---|

| [MirthlyStudio](https://www.etsy.com/shop/MirthlyStudio) | ~34.4k sales, 1,108 items, 4.9 rating [[21]](../references.md#ref-21) | Product families, bundles, websites, add-ons and cross-selling |

## Current observations

### Invitations can become product families

MirthlyStudio structures its catalogue around invitations plus bundles, games, signs, websites and extras. [[21]](../references.md#ref-21) This is more interesting economically than evaluating only one invitation price.

### Cultural segmentation is a real strategy

JoyablyStudio separates Muslim, Sikh, Hindu/Indian, Jewish and other cultural occasions alongside general celebrations. [[20]](../references.md#ref-20) This supports investigating cultural and language long-tail opportunities.

### Price competition is aggressive

DigitalbloomInvite currently uses substantial discounts and low single-digit prices across many invitation listings. [[22]](../references.md#ref-22) Competing only on price is unattractive.

### Large bundles require caution

ZenovaVibe currently promotes very large PLR/MRR and colouring/education bundles. [[23]](../references.md#ref-23) Etsy's Creativity Standards state that seller-designed digital content must be the seller's own creative work; bundles or PDFs of someone else's work do not qualify. [[7]](../references.md#ref-7)

We can study the commercial presentation without assuming its content model is appropriate for us.

## Review research

For each serious opportunity, code only themes that affect product decisions:

| Theme | Evidence to capture |
|---|---|
| Editing | Canva difficulty, mobile editing, confusing fields |
| Delivery | Download/access issues, instructions |
| Format | Print sizes, mobile versions, PDF/PNG needs |
| Personalisation | What buyers expect to change |
| Bundle | Missing matching products or extras |
| Language / culture | Translation, terminology, cultural fit |
| Quality | Design, pedagogy, print/usability |
| Requests | Products buyers explicitly request |

For shortlisted niches, analyse roughly 10–15 relevant shops/listings, mixing established, mid-sized and newer sellers.

A small number of selected competitor products can be purchased to evaluate:

> **purchase → download → instructions → edit → use/export → final experience**

The objective is customer-experience research, not copying.
