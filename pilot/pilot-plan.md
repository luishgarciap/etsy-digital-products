## Pilot design

The first pilot should be large enough to generate useful evidence but small enough to change direction quickly.

Working design:

- **3–5 strong products** from the selected family;
- variants or bundles only when they test a specific hypothesis;
- product/search terms derived from Marketplace Insights;
- no broad catalogue build before evidence;
- no paid advertising by default in the first observation period.

Etsy itself states that Ads work best when listings already receive buyer interest and are unlikely to turn a low-demand listing into a top seller. [[24]](../references.md#ref-24)

## Observation period

A first review after roughly **30–45 days** is the current working starting point. This is a project decision, not an Etsy rule. The period can be adjusted for seasonality, traffic level or the nature of the product.

## Pilot outcomes

After measurement and learning, each product/family should move to one of these actions:

- **EXPAND** — strong evidence; build adjacent products/bundles;
- **IMPROVE** — promising signal but execution problem;
- **REPRICE / REPACKAGE** — demand exists but conversion/economics are weak;
- **PAUSE** — insufficient evidence or lower priority;
- **REJECT** — hypothesis not supported.

Discovery ends when the product-family hypotheses are sufficiently resolved to move into normal operation, or when we decide there is no viable path worth pursuing.
