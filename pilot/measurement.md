## Core measures

| Area | Measures |
|---|---|
| Discovery | Listing views, shop visits, Etsy-search traffic, favourites |
| Conversion | Orders, conversion rate, orders per listing |
| Commercial | Revenue, average order value, contribution after platform/payment costs |
| Product | Performance by product family, single item vs bundle |
| Production | Creation time, reusable assets, support time |
| Customer | Questions, reviews, requests and complaints |
