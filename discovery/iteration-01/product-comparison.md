This is the current cross-category view. A numeric ranking would be premature while key evidence remains missing.

| Category | Demand/search evidence | Competitor/product evidence | Buyer/review evidence | Economic clarity | Most important unresolved question |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Invitations / Celebrations | Large demand is clear; broad competition is high; selected Etsy validation is pending | Strong: coordinated suites, large bundles and cultural specialists | Substantial positive evidence; critical product feedback is thinner | Partial: bundle/support effort is not yet quantified | Which narrower occasion/segment gives a realistic entry point? |
| Café / Gastronomy / Bakery | `coffee menu` / `matcha menu` are interesting; bakery and broader café evidence is thinner | Relevant models identified: focused menu, multi-layout offer and broader business kit | Product-specific menu reviews are thinner than in the stronger categories | Partial: creation effort by product model is not yet measured | Simple menu, multi-layout or broader business kit? |
| Bilingual Children / Family | Strong Spanish screening; Etsy validation and Portuguese-English evidence are thinner | Good: smaller thematic resources and large learning systems are represented | Spanish/bilingual product-specific reviews remain incomplete | Partial: low prices must be weighed against QA and bundle effort | Can bundle value overcome low prices and QA effort? |
| Catholic / Faith | Broad search evidence exists; selected prayer/communion searches still need Etsy validation | Good: concise guide, activity and scale/volume models are represented | Concrete buyer-use insights; critical evidence remains thinner | Partial: one-sheet versus bundle economics are not yet clear | Which prayer/sacrament/bilingual need is commercially strongest? |
| Colouring / Creative | Strong broad search evidence; narrower hand-drawn searches remain less clear | Strong: three distinct production models are documented | Strong positive evidence; critical quality/use feedback is thinner | Partial: original-art production throughput is unmeasured | Can original work be produced at a commercially realistic rate? |

## Current read

- No category should be declared the winner yet.
- Invitations, Catholic and Colouring currently have particularly useful competitor/review learning.
- Café still deserves serious consideration because its screening economics are interesting, but the buyer-evidence base needs strengthening.
- Bilingual learning remains credible, but low unit prices make production architecture and bundling central.
- Colouring has the clearest comparison of operating models, but original-art throughput is still unknown.

## Evidence still incomplete

- Etsy Marketplace Insights results for the selected searches;
- the category-specific buyer/review gaps documented in [Opportunity assessment](opportunity-assessment.md#research-gaps--exact-meaning);
- production-time/economic estimates for the leading product models.

Direct product inspection remains available within competitor analysis where public listing/review evidence is insufficient.
