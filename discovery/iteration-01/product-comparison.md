## Current cross-category observations

- Invitations combines large reported demand with high reported competition in broad searches and several narrower competitor models.
- Café currently has measured coffee/matcha search data and three different competitor offer models, with fewer product-specific menu reviews in the current evidence base.
- Bilingual learning includes both small-resource and large-system models; current screening prices are relatively low and QA/bundle effort remains only partially measured.
- Catholic research includes concrete buyer-use evidence across guides, prayer resources and children's activities, while selected Etsy search validation is not yet collected.
- Colouring has three documented production models, while original-art production throughput is not yet measured.

## Evidence not yet collected

- Etsy Marketplace Insights results for the selected searches;
- the category-specific evidence gaps documented in [Opportunity assessment](opportunity-assessment.md#research-gaps--exact-meaning);
- production-time/economic estimates for selected product models.
