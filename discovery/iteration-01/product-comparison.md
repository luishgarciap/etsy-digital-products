## Comparison rule

Candidates must be compared on the **same evidence dimensions**. Search volume alone is not a decision rule.

## Decision criteria

1. Buyer demand
2. Competition / discoverability
3. Buyer intent and unmet need
4. Price / contribution
5. Production effort
6. Reusability / bundle potential
7. Customer-support burden
8. IP / licensing / platform risk
9. Ability to build a coherent product family
10. Strength and quality of evidence

## Comparison complete when

- every shortlisted candidate has comparable official demand data;
- at least one credible unmet need is identified;
- candidate price/effort economics are estimated;
- platform/licensing risks are understood;
- we can explain why the top candidate is stronger than the alternatives.
