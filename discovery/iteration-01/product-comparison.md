This is the current cross-category view. A numeric ranking would be premature while key evidence remains missing.

| Category | Demand/search evidence | Competitor/product evidence | Buyer/review evidence | Economic clarity | Most important unresolved question |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Invitations / Celebrations | **Strong** | **Strong** | Positive evidence strong; critical reviews thinner | **Moderate** | Which narrower occasion/segment gives a realistic entry point? |
| Café / Gastronomy / Bakery | **Good but incomplete** | **Moderate** | **Weakest product-specific review evidence** | **Moderate** | Simple menu, multi-layout or broader business kit? |
| Bilingual Children / Family | **Strong screening** | **Good** | **Moderate** | **Moderate-low** | Can bundle value overcome low prices and QA effort? |
| Catholic / Faith | **Good** | **Good** | **Strong / actionable** | **Moderate-low** | How large are the strongest prayer/sacrament/bilingual needs? |
| Colouring / Creative | **Strong** | **Strong** | **Strong** | **Low-moderate** | Can original work be produced at a commercially realistic rate? |

## Current read

- No category should be declared the winner yet.
- Invitations, Catholic and Colouring currently have particularly useful competitor/review learning.
- Café still deserves serious consideration because its screening economics are interesting, but the buyer-evidence base needs strengthening.
- Bilingual learning remains credible, but low unit prices make production architecture and bundling central.
- Colouring has the clearest comparison of operating models, but original-art throughput is still unknown.

## Evidence still needed before pilot selection

- 15 Etsy Marketplace Insights results;
- the most important pending RankHero checks;
- deliberate low-star review findings;
- observations from a small set of purchased competitor products;
- initial production-time/economic estimates for the leading product concepts.
