## Decision question

> **Which initial digital-product opportunity offers the strongest combination of buyer demand, unmet need, viable economics and repeatable production, and therefore deserves the first controlled pilot?**

## Current scope

Iteration 01 compares five directions:

1. **Café / small-business resources** — menus, price lists, signs, loyalty materials and coherent business kits.
2. **Invitations / celebrations** — editable invitations and related event bundles.
3. **Bilingual learning** — Spanish-English / Portuguese-English learning resources and family materials.
4. **Catholic / faith-based products** — children, First Communion, Confirmation, prayer/Rosary, bilingual and occasion resources.
5. **Colouring / creative products** — only where a specific buyer/use case can be identified.

## Starting position

We already have screening evidence for café/gastronomy, invitations and bilingual learning. Café-specific keywords and Spanish flashcards currently look more attractive than generic invitations, but those signals rely partly on third-party keyword estimates and must be checked against Etsy's own Marketplace Insights data. [[13]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-13) [[14]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-14) [[15]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-15) [[16]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-16)

Catholic and colouring products have not yet received equivalent demand screening, so they should not be unfairly ranked below the original three.

## What Iteration 01 must establish

By the end of the investigation we should understand:

- which search terms show meaningful buyer demand;
- how much competing supply exists;
- which buyer needs or complaints recur;
- what products/bundles buyers already pay for;
- what production effort and policy/licensing constraints apply;
- whether the price/fee structure can support sensible contribution;
- which candidate(s) deserve the first pilot.

## Evidence standard

Official Etsy data takes priority where available. Third-party keyword data is used for screening and prioritisation, not as final proof of demand.

## Exit from FRAME

FRAME is complete. Iteration 01 is now in **INVESTIGATE**.
