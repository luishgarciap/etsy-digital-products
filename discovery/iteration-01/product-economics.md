## Initial test-price hypotheses

Proposed before market research; these are not observed averages:

| Product type | Initial test range | Original reasoning |
|---|---:|---|
| Individual card | US$3–5 | Fixed per-order fees matter at low prices |
| Editable invitation | US$4–9 | Design and editing experience matter |
| Coordinated event bundle | US$9–19 | May increase average order value |
| Café / business kit | US$9–25 | Higher value if it saves real work |
| Children's learning bundle | US$5–15 | Requires content and quality review |

Current implications:

- simple flashcards are more attractive if production is reusable and products can be bundled;
- café/matcha products show stronger single-item contribution in the current screen, but production effort is still unknown;
- generic invitations combine low price points with very high competing supply;
- Catholic and colouring products need comparable price/product evidence before economics can be judged.
