| Candidate | Illustrative selling price | Approx. platform/payment fees* | Approx. contribution* |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Coffee menu | US$7.00 | US$1.41 | US$5.59 |
| Matcha menu | US$6.85 | US$1.39 | US$5.46 |
| Birthday invitation | US$5.12 | US$1.17 | US$3.95 |
| Spanish flashcards | US$3.40 | US$0.94 | US$2.46 |

\*Approximation before Payoneer/FX, tax, software, refunds, ads and production/support time. Based on current Etsy fee rules. [[2]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-2) [[3]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-3)

These figures do **not** establish profitability. Production time and bundle structure still need to be measured.

## What this implies

- Simple bilingual flashcards are likely to require efficient production or larger packs/bundles because the observed median price is low.
- Café/matcha products show better single-item contribution at current screening prices, but the opportunity still depends on demand quality and production effort.
- Generic invitations face both lower price points and very heavy competition; coordinated bundles or long-tail segments may be more economically interesting.
- Catholic and colouring economics cannot be compared fairly until price/product formats are investigated.
