## How to use this file

Use this page for **shops, listings, reviews, product structures, representative products and possible research-purchase candidates**. Demand metrics belong in [Market & demand](market-demand.md); financial modelling belongs in [Product economics](product-economics.md).

**Jump to:** [Invitations](#invitations--celebrations) · [Café](#café--gastronomy--bakery) · [Bilingual](#bilingual-children--family-learning) · [Catholic](#catholic--faith-based) · [Colouring](#colouring--creative) · [Representative products](#representative-product-shortlist) · [References](../../knowledge/references.md)

### Competitor-entry standard

Every competitor entry should contain, as far as the current evidence allows:

> **Shop name + direct Etsy link → why we selected it → relevant offering in our category → 1–3 concrete products with direct links → price / formats / bundle structure → positive review findings → critical review findings → exact review/listing source → what it teaches us → whether direct product inspection could add evidence, and why.**

For fields without research evidence, write **not yet collected / not yet verified** and use observed evidence only.

**Direct product inspection:** optional competitor-analysis method for product-level questions requiring access beyond public listings and reviews.

**Review-source standard:** every review finding must point to the Etsy shop/listing/review source in the master IEEE-style bibliography in [References](../../knowledge/references.md). Label **Etsy's own AI review summary** explicitly and distinguish it from manually reviewed comments.

**Price-recording standard:** Etsy prices and discounts move frequently. Record a price when a dated observation is available; otherwise use **price snapshot not yet captured** and keep the linked listing as the source to check at purchase time.

### Competitor roles

- **Core competitor:** repeatedly relevant or strongly representative of the category.
- **Specialist competitor:** particularly strong in one meaningful sub-niche.
- **Benchmark:** useful operating/product model to learn from.
- **Contrast case:** deliberately different model used to understand alternative strategies and trade-offs.

Public Etsy sales counts are cumulative; monthly revenue, cost and profit are unavailable from those counts. Shop-level ratings provide shop-wide evidence, while product-level reviews provide evidence about the specific product type being studied.

# Invitations / Celebrations

## InvitationDesignStd — core candidate

**Shop:** [InvitationDesignStd on Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/shop/InvitationDesignStd) [[38]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-38)

**Why selected:** strong example of a coordinated invitation **suite** rather than a single invitation. It helps us test whether completeness and consistency create enough buyer value to justify a larger bundle.

**Relevant offering:** editable wedding stationery and coordinated event suites.

**Representative product:**

- [Luxury Burgundy Wedding Invitation Suite](https://www.etsy.com/in-en/listing/4590477604/luxury-burgundy-wedding-invitation-suite) [[39]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-39)

**Price / format / bundle:** current public listing identifies a digital download delivered through a PDF and editable Canva workflow. The suite includes the main invitation, details card, RSVP/monogram/place card, welcome-party card and envelope liner; exports include PDF/JPEG/PNG. Text, fonts, colours and text placement are editable, while artwork/page sizes are more constrained. **The displayed price is promotion-sensitive and may change.** [[39]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-39)

**Positive review findings:** product-specific review evidence currently captured for this listing is limited. Shop-level reputation is strong; suite-specific buyer feedback remains limited. [[38]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-38), [[39]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-39)

**Critical review findings:** additional critical-review evidence has not yet been collected on editing friction, non-editable artwork, resizing, printing, instructions and export quality.

**Exact evidence source:** shop [[38]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-38); representative listing [[39]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-39).

**What it teaches us:** the product unit can extend from a single invitation to an event-wide **product family/bundle**.

**Hands-on research value:** **High.** Hands-on inspection can reveal Canva source-file structure, instructions, organisation and export workflow.

## Direct product inspection checklist

If a product is purchased for direct inspection, record:

- listing and price paid;
- what the buyer actually receives;
- download process;
- instructions/onboarding;
- Canva/editing experience where relevant;
- mobile vs desktop usability;
- export/print quality;
- file organisation and naming;
- what feels polished;
- what is confusing or missing;
- what buyer problem the product solves;
- ideas for a genuinely different/better product while preserving original content and design.
