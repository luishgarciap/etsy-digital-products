## Current decision state

**Pilot selection remains open.** All five have search, competitor/product and review evidence. The sections below distinguish current evidence from additional evidence that could refine the comparison.

## Invitations / Celebrations

**Evidence currently available**

- RankHero screening reports approximately 301k monthly searches for `wedding invitation`, 90.5k for `birthday invitations` and 40.5k for `baby shower invites`. [[74]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-74) [[27]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-27) [[79]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-79)
- Broad competition is also high in the current screening, including approximately 930k for `wedding invitation` and 1.24m for `birthday invitations`. [[74]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-74) [[27]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-27)
- Search discovery includes narrower occasion, format and cultural directions.
- Competitor research includes coordinated product families, bundles and culturally specific offers. [[38]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-38) [[35]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-35) [[34]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-34)

**Evidence not yet collected**

- Etsy Marketplace Insights results for the selected searches.
- A larger sample of critical product reviews on editing, mobile use, resizing, printing and instructions.

**Current interpretation:** broad generic invitations combine large reported search demand with high reported competition; narrower occasion, cultural and coordinated-product-family propositions remain candidates for comparison.

## Café / Gastronomy / Bakery

**Evidence currently available**

- RankHero reports approximately 18k–22k monthly searches and 4,368 competition for `coffee menu`. [[24]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-24)
- RankHero reports approximately 4.4k monthly searches and 191 competition for `matcha menu`. [[25]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-25)
- Current competitor research includes a focused menu, a multi-layout offer and a broader business-resource model. [[46]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-46) [[48]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-48) [[50]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-50)
- Current screening prices are approximately US$7.00 for `coffee menu`, US$6.85 for `matcha menu` and US$4.99 for `food menu template`. [[24]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-24) [[25]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-25) [[26]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-26)

**Evidence not yet collected**

- More demand evidence for bakery and broader café terms.
- More product-specific menu reviews.
- Production-effort evidence for a focused menu, multi-layout offer and broader business kit.

**Current interpretation:** the category contains at least three distinct offer models. Buyer-value and production-economics evidence is not yet comparable across those models.

## Bilingual Children / Family Learning

**Evidence currently available**

- RankHero reports approximately 6.6k monthly searches and 3.1k competition for `spanish flashcards`. [[28]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-28)
- RankHero reports approximately 135k monthly searches and 2.5k competition for `spanish alphabet`; this large reported search/competition difference has not yet been checked in Etsy Marketplace Insights. [[80]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-80)
- Competitor research includes both small thematic resources and large worksheet/learning systems. [[52]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-52) [[53]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-53) [[55]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-55)
- Current screening prices are approximately US$3.40 for `spanish flashcards` and US$4.99 for `spanish alphabet`. [[28]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-28) [[80]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-80)

**Evidence not yet collected**

- More reviews specifically tied to Spanish/bilingual products.
- Portuguese-English demand evidence.
- More complete QA-effort and bundle-economics evidence.

**Current interpretation:** the category supports both small-resource and large-system product architectures; the relative economics depend on price, reuse, bundling and QA effort.

## Catholic / Faith-based

**Evidence currently available**

- RankHero reports approximately 60.5k monthly searches for `catholic prayer`, 18.1k for `prayer cards` and 8.1k for `bible coloring pages`. [[81]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-81) [[82]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-82) [[83]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-83)
- Review evidence identifies buyer-use themes including portability, concise information, easy printing and clear instructions. [[60]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-60) [[61]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-61)
- One buyer review provides a Spanish→English Catholic-prayer use case. [[60]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-60)
- Competitor research includes concise guides, prayer resources, children's activities and larger activity/game catalogues. [[59]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-59) [[61]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-61) [[63]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-63)

**Evidence not yet collected**

- Etsy Marketplace Insights results for selected prayer/communion searches.
- Broader evidence on bilingual and sacramental demand.
- More critical reviews and additional price/value evidence across single-resource and bundle formats.

**Current interpretation:** several product forms are represented in the evidence across prayer, sacramental, bilingual and children's sub-directions; selection remains open.

## Colouring / Creative

**Evidence currently available**

- RankHero reports approximately 110k monthly searches and 82k competition for `cute coloring pages`. [[84]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-84)
- `cozy coloring book` is reported at approximately 33k–40k monthly searches and 31k–53k competition, while `printable coloring pages` is reported at approximately 135k monthly searches and 512k competition. [[85]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-85) [[86]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-86)
- Competitor research documents three production models: larger catalogue/frequent releases, small original hand-drawn catalogue, and recognisable branded product families. [[65]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-65) [[67]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-67) [[70]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-70)
- Buyer reviews include evidence on downloads, print/digital use, line style, variety and repeat purchase. [[65]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-65) [[71]](../../knowledge/references.md#ref-71)

**Evidence not yet collected**

- Etsy Marketplace Insights results for the selected searches.
- Narrower search evidence for terms such as `hand drawn coloring`.
- More critical reviews on line quality, resolution, printing and tablet use.
- Additional evidence on original-art production effort and throughput.

**Current interpretation:** three distinct production models are documented for comparison; the economics of original hand-drawn production are not yet measured.

## Research gaps — exact meaning

Evidence not yet collected that could improve the comparison between opportunities:

| Category | Evidence not yet collected | Why it may matter |
|---|---|---|
| **Invitations / Celebrations** | A larger sample of **critical / 1–3-star reviews**. | Critical reviews can reveal recurring friction in Canva editing, mobile use, resizing, printing, instructions, non-editable artwork, support expectations and mismatch between previews and delivered files. |
| **Café / Gastronomy / Bakery** | More product-level reviews for specific café/menu/bakery products, plus more demand evidence for bakery and broader café terms. | Product-level feedback can show how buyers experience editing, sizes, printing, readability, price updates, logo/image replacement, instructions and business use; broader search evidence can improve comparison across café/bakery sub-directions. |
| **Bilingual Children / Family Learning** | More reviews tied specifically to **Spanish/bilingual products**, plus Portuguese-English demand evidence. | This can add evidence on age fit, linguistic accuracy, printing/organisation, completeness and whether large bundles feel structured or overwhelming. |
| **Catholic / Faith-based** | Etsy validation for selected searches; broader bilingual/sacramental evidence; more critical reviews; more single-resource versus bundle price/value evidence. | This can improve comparison across prayer guidance, bilingual resources, sacramental material and children's activities. |
| **Colouring / Creative** | Etsy validation for narrower searches such as `hand drawn coloring`; more critical reviews on line quality/resolution/printing/tablet use; page-count/PDF+PNG value evidence; original-art production-effort estimates. | This can improve comparison of catalogue breadth, perceived product value and the economics of an original hand-drawn model. |

## Additional evidence that may affect the comparison

1. **Etsy Marketplace Insights** — could strengthen or weaken the current demand/competition signals for selected searches.
2. **Additional product-specific buyer feedback** — could reveal recurring problems, unmet needs or differentiation opportunities across categories.
3. **Direct product inspection, where useful** — could provide evidence on actual product quality, usability, organisation and perceived value that is unavailable from public listings and reviews.
4. **Production-time and economic estimates** — could change the relative attractiveness of product models once creation effort, reuse, support and contribution per hour are considered.
