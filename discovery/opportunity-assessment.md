## Current assessment

This assessment integrates the evidence available **before** the first official Marketplace Insights search set is run. It is therefore a current working synthesis, not the final product selection.

### Café / gastronomy — **promising, specific sub-niches preferred**

`coffee menu` and especially `matcha menu` have attractive third-party demand/competition screening signals, while the broader `food menu template` term is materially more crowded. [[10]](../references.md#ref-10) [[11]](../references.md#ref-11) [[12]](../references.md#ref-12)

The opportunity should be refined toward specific café/bakery/matcha use cases and coherent business kits rather than a generic restaurant-menu template.

### Bilingual learning — **promising, economics depend on product depth**

`spanish flashcards` combines strong third-party demand with relatively low competition, and Etsy's public market shows established bilingual products with substantial review histories. [[14]](../references.md#ref-14) [[17]](../references.md#ref-17)

The main constraint is low simple-product pricing. The stronger model may be themed packs, larger learning bundles or products with clearer educational value.

### Invitations / celebrations — **proven demand, structurally crowded**

Generic birthday invitations show very large demand but more than one million competing listings in the third-party screening. [[13]](../references.md#ref-13) Established shops demonstrate that cultural segmentation and coordinated product families can work. [[19]](../references.md#ref-19) [[20]](../references.md#ref-20)

The opportunity should therefore be narrowed before comparison: culture, language, occasion, format or bundle—not generic invitations.

### Catholic / faith-based — **active market, insufficient demand evidence**

The public `Catholic prayer cards` market shows 5,000+ items and meaningful review activity across children's resources, prayer guides and printables. [[18]](../references.md#ref-18)

That is enough to justify investigation, but not enough to conclude that the category has attractive demand-to-competition economics. Marketplace Insights should determine whether children, sacramental occasions, bilingual products or another sub-niche merits comparison.

### Colouring / creative — **not yet comparable**

Colouring is currently a production/product idea rather than a sufficiently framed opportunity. A buyer, use case and specific search space should be defined before it enters the comparison stage.

## Refined opportunity set for the next stage

Subject to official Marketplace Insights verification, the candidates currently strong enough to carry forward are:

1. matcha / specific café menu products;
2. coffee / bakery small-business kits;
3. Spanish-English learning packs;
4. a narrow invitation sub-niche identified through long-tail evidence;
5. a Catholic sub-niche only if Marketplace Insights produces a credible demand signal.

Colouring stays in FRAME/INVESTIGATE until it is defined more precisely.

## Evidence required before final comparison

- official 30-day Etsy search/listing data for the priority keywords;
- targeted recent review mining for the strongest terms;
- observed product/bundle price ranges;
- production-effort estimate for candidate products;
- unit economics using current Etsy fees.
