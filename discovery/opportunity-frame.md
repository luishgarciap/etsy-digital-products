## Current objective

Build an Etsy digital-products business that can identify products with real buyer demand and produce them profitably and repeatedly.

The project is based in Brazil and aimed at international buyers. We are not setting a fixed long-term income target at this stage; the first task is to establish a repeatable product opportunity and learn from real market behaviour.

## Current opportunity question

> **Which digital-product opportunity should we test first, for which buyer and use case, at what price/bundle, and with what production model?**

## Candidate directions

The current candidate set is:

- small-business / café / gastronomy resources;
- invitations and celebration products;
- bilingual children and family learning materials;
- Catholic / faith-based products;
- colouring / creative products;
- stronger opportunities discovered during research.

The first three originated in the initial study and have preliminary screening data. Catholic and colouring were added later and need comparable evidence before they can be judged alongside the original candidates.

## Investigation scope for this cycle

This cycle will determine:

1. where observable buyer demand exists;
2. how much competing supply exists;
3. what buyers value or complain about;
4. what product formats, bundles and prices are common;
5. whether platform fees leave acceptable economics;
6. whether products can be produced legally, efficiently and repeatedly;
7. which candidates deserve a controlled pilot.

The frame will be revised only after the evidence has been synthesised in [Opportunity assessment](opportunity-assessment.md) or after a pilot creates new learning.
