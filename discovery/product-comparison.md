## Comparison criteria for the decision gate

Before moving to DECIDE, compare the strongest candidates on:

- direct Etsy demand;
- competing listings and seller maturity;
- buyer intent and review evidence;
- unmet need;
- price and bundle potential;
- expected contribution after Etsy/payment costs;
- production time and repeatability;
- content/IP/licensing risk;
- ability to create a coherent first product family;
- evidence confidence.

The purpose of COMPARE is not to force every idea into a score. It is to make the trade-offs visible enough that the pilot choice can be justified.
