## Executive conclusion

There is no current platform-level blocker to using **Brazil as the operating base while selling to international Etsy buyers**. Brazil is eligible for Etsy Payments through Payoneer. [[5]](../../references.md#ref-5) [[4]](../../references.md#ref-4)

## Marketplace scale

At 30 September 2026, Etsy reported approximately **87.5 million active buyers** and **5.836 million active sellers**. [[9]](../../references.md#ref-9) These figures establish marketplace scale; they do not prove that a new digital-products shop will be profitable.

## Brazil and Payoneer

For sellers in Brazil, Etsy Payments uses a Payoneer Payment Account. Etsy states that available funds are sent to Payoneer each Monday, deposits are in USD, and bank withdrawal typically takes 2–5 business days after the withdrawal request. [[4]](../../references.md#ref-4)

Detailed account arrangements can be finalised later; they are not part of product discovery.

## Digital delivery

Etsy supports instant-download and made-to-order digital products. Instant-download listings can contain **up to five files, maximum 20 MB each**, and digital products must be made and/or designed by the seller. [[6]](../../references.md#ref-6)

This affects how editable templates, instructions and larger asset packages are delivered.

## Canva

Canva allows original designs and templates to be sold under its licence conditions. Canva states that templates must be original and that Pro Content used in templates may only be sold when the template is shared as a Canva template link; Pro Content cannot simply be redistributed as standalone content. [[25]](../../references.md#ref-25)

This must be checked product-by-product before launch.

## AI-assisted creation

Etsy permits AI in the creative process subject to its current rules. Its published guidance prohibits AI prompt bundles and requires listing images/content to represent the actual item honestly; other Etsy IP and content rules continue to apply. [[8]](../../references.md#ref-8) Etsy's Creativity Standards remain the primary originality reference. [[7]](../../references.md#ref-7)

For this project:

> **buyer need → product concept → original creative process → appropriate tools**

AI is a production capability, not the product strategy.

## Search and discovery

Etsy search works through query matching and ranking. Etsy's seller guidance emphasises relevant keywords, categories/attributes and listing/shop quality signals. [[26]](../../references.md#ref-26)

Marketplace Insights is the preferred source for direct buyer-search evidence because it reports 30-day Etsy searches and listings for queried terms. [[1]](../../references.md#ref-1)

## Advertising

Etsy Ads currently require a minimum daily budget of US$1, and new shops must wait 15 days before starting a campaign. Etsy explicitly advises that ads work best when listings already receive buyer interest and are unlikely to turn a low-demand listing into a top seller. [[24]](../../references.md#ref-24)

The first pilot should therefore establish product/search fit before relying on paid promotion.

## Pre-launch constraints to carry forward

- originality and IP;
- Canva/content licensing;
- AI policy compliance;
- low-price fee sensitivity;
- digital file limits and buyer instructions;
- search relevance and listing quality;
- Payoneer/FX costs;
- Brazil tax and local legal obligations before formal operation.
