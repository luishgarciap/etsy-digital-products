\*RankHero's page contains a dynamic competition figure; its country competition table currently reports 3,083. Treat third-party counts as screening data, not audited Etsy totals.

Public Etsy market pages add supply and buyer-activity context:

- baby-shower invitation templates: **5,000+ items**, with strong review counts and both low-price single invites and higher-value bundles/web invitations. [[15]](../../references.md#ref-15)
- bakery menu templates: **1,000+/2,000+ results depending market page**, including menus, price lists, business bundles and operational spreadsheets. [[16]](../../references.md#ref-16)
- bilingual flash cards: **1,000+ items**, including low-price printables and established Montessori/bilingual resources. [[17]](../../references.md#ref-17)
- Catholic prayer cards: **5,000+ items**, including children's resources, Rosary guides and prayer printables with substantial review histories. [[18]](../../references.md#ref-18)

These public result counts show supply and visible buyer activity; they do not measure search demand.

## Preferred evidence source: Etsy Marketplace Insights

Marketplace Insights provides direct Etsy data for a keyword, including searches and listings in the last 30 days plus related search terms. Sellers currently receive **15 free keyword searches per week**, and results remain available for seven days. [[1]](../../references.md#ref-1)
