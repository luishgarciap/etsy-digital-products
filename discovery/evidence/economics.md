## Current Etsy cost structure

For a Brazil-based Etsy Payments seller, the main platform charges relevant to digital products are:

| Cost | Current rule |
|---|---|
| Shop set-up | One-time, non-refundable fee; amount varies by location and is shown during set-up [[2]](../../references.md#ref-2) |
| Listing | US$0.20 per listing; a listing expires after four months [[2]](../../references.md#ref-2) |
| Transaction | 6.5% of total order amount [[2]](../../references.md#ref-2) |
| Etsy Payments — Brazil | 6.5% + US$0.30 per order [[3]](../../references.md#ref-3) |
| Currency conversion | 2.5% when Etsy must convert listing currency into payment-account currency [[2]](../../references.md#ref-2) |
| Offsite Ads | 15% on attributed orders below the US$10k threshold; 12% after threshold; participation rules differ by threshold [[23]](../../references.md#ref-23) |
| Etsy Ads | Optional CPC ads; minimum daily budget US$1; new shops wait 15 days [[24]](../../references.md#ref-24) |

Payoneer may add withdrawal minimums and fees. [[4]](../../references.md#ref-4)

## Illustrative US$5 sale

Ignoring tax, Payoneer/FX, software, refunds and labour:

- Sale price: **US$5.00**
- Etsy transaction fee (6.5%): **−US$0.325**
- Brazil payment processing (6.5% + US$0.30): **−US$0.625**
- Listing/renewal allocation: **−US$0.20**
- Approximate remainder before other costs: **US$3.85**

If the sale were attributed to a 15% Offsite Ad, another US$0.75 would reduce the illustrative remainder to about **US$3.10**. [[2]](../../references.md#ref-2) [[3]](../../references.md#ref-3) [[23]](../../references.md#ref-23)

This is contribution before other costs, **not net profit**.

## Price signals already observed

- `coffee menu`: median/typical price around **US$7** in RankHero's sample. [[10]](../../references.md#ref-10)
- `matcha menu`: around **US$6.85**. [[11]](../../references.md#ref-11)
- `spanish flashcards`: around **US$3.40**. [[14]](../../references.md#ref-14)
- `birthday invitations`: around **US$5.12**. [[13]](../../references.md#ref-13)

These prices make production reuse, bundles and low support effort strategically important.
