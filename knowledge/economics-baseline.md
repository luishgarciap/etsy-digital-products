## Etsy fee model relevant to Brazil

| Cost | Current baseline |
|---|---|
| Listing | US$0.20 per listing; listing renewals/quantities can create further listing fees [[2]](references.md#ref-2) |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% of the total order amount [[2]](references.md#ref-2) |
| Etsy Payments — Brazil | 6.5% + US$0.30 per order [[3]](references.md#ref-3) |
| Payoneer | Additional withdrawal/FX costs may apply; Etsy deposits to Payoneer in USD [[4]](references.md#ref-4) |
| Offsite Ads | 15% on attributed orders while the shop remains below the US$10,000 threshold; 12% after the threshold, when participation becomes mandatory [[11]](references.md#ref-11) |
| Etsy Ads | Optional CPC advertising; minimum US$1/day after the initial 15-day wait [[12]](references.md#ref-12) |

## Reusable US$5 illustration

Before tax, Payoneer/FX, software, labour and other costs:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Selling price | US$5.00 |
| Transaction fee (6.5%) | −US$0.325 |
| Brazil processing (6.5% + US$0.30) | −US$0.625 |
| Listing/renewal allocation | −US$0.20 |
| **Approx. contribution before other costs** | **US$3.85** |

If a sale is attributed to a 15% Offsite Ad, the same illustrative order would lose a further US$0.75, leaving about **US$3.10** before other costs. [[2]](references.md#ref-2) [[3]](references.md#ref-3) [[11]](references.md#ref-11)

This is not net profit.

## Measures to keep separate

1. Revenue
2. Contribution after Etsy/payment fees
3. Profit after tools, advertising and operating costs
4. Profit after tax

## Decision principle

Low-priced digital products are more attractive when production effort can be reused across related products, bundles raise order value, and support remains low.

Product-specific prices, creation effort and contribution belong in the iteration's `product-economics.md`, not here.
