## Etsy fee structure — Brazil

| Cost | Current rule |
|---|---|
| Shop set-up | One-time non-refundable fee; amount varies and is shown during shop setup [[17]](references.md#ref-17) |
| Listing / renewal | US$0.20 per listing; renewal can create another US$0.20 listing fee [[17]](references.md#ref-17) |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% of total order amount [[17]](references.md#ref-17) |
| Etsy Payments — Brazil | 6.5% + US$0.30 per order [[18]](references.md#ref-18) |
| Currency conversion | 2.5% when Etsy converts between shop and payment-account currencies [[17]](references.md#ref-17) |
| Payoneer | Withdrawal/FX charges depend on account/corridor; current fees must be confirmed in the actual account [[9]](references.md#ref-9) [[10]](references.md#ref-10) |
| Etsy Ads | Optional CPC advertising; minimum US$1/day after the initial 15-day wait [[12]](references.md#ref-12) |
| Offsite Ads | 15% on attributed orders while the shop is below the US$10,000 threshold; 12% after the threshold, subject to Etsy's rules [[19]](references.md#ref-19) |

## The US$0.20 listing rule

A listing lasts four months. If it stays active and renews, another listing fee is charged. Digital products can be sold repeatedly, but listing/renewal fees are therefore not necessarily a one-time lifetime cost. [[17]](references.md#ref-17)

### Catalogue cost with no sales

| Active catalogue | Initial listing | Month 4 renewal | Month 8 renewal | Total before month 12 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 listings | US$2 | US$2 | US$2 | US$6 |
| 20 listings | US$4 | US$4 | US$4 | US$12 |
| 400 listings | US$80 | US$80 | US$80 | US$240 |

This is one reason to start with a small catalogue and expand after evidence appears.

## US$5 product example

Before Payoneer/FX, tax, software, refunds, labour and other costs:

| Item | No paid ad | 15% Offsite Ad |
|---|---:|---:|
| Selling price | US$5.00 | US$5.00 |
| Etsy transaction fee (6.5%) | −US$0.325 | −US$0.325 |
| Brazil payment processing (6.5% + US$0.30) | −US$0.625 | −US$0.625 |
| Listing / renewal allocation | −US$0.20 | −US$0.20 |
| Attributed Offsite Ad | — | −US$0.75 |
| **Illustrative remainder** | **US$3.85** | **US$3.10** |

Under the same simplified assumptions:

- 100 sales × US$5 = US$500 revenue and about **US$385** after those basic Etsy/payment charges;
- roughly **130 sales** would be needed to leave around US$500 after those same basic charges.

These are contribution illustrations, not net profit.

## Advertising risk illustration

If ten paid clicks cost US$0.30 each and only one generates a US$5 sale, advertising spend would be US$3 and only about US$0.85 would remain after the same basic Etsy/payment charges.

This is **not an Etsy CPC forecast**. It illustrates why advertising economics should be measured rather than assumed.

## Payoneer / USD → BRL

An earlier working assumption used up to roughly 2% for some USD→BRL Payoneer withdrawal arrangements. Payoneer's current public pricing is corridor/account dependent and can differ, so the exact fee must be confirmed in the actual Payoneer account before launch. [[10]](references.md#ref-10)

## UK reference — not the base plan

UK fees as a reference point:

- UK Etsy Payments processing: **4% + £0.20 per order**; [[18]](references.md#ref-18)
- UK Regulatory Operating Fee: **0.48%**. [[20]](references.md#ref-20)

Brazil has higher payment-processing fees, but the operating plan remains a genuine Brazil-based setup. Opening in the UK purely to reduce fees could create ownership, tax and operational complications.

## Preliminary startup-budget assumption

Historical startup assumption:

- 15 listings → US$3 initial listing fees;
- plus the shop setup fee shown by Etsy;
- a suggested **US$30–60 ceiling** for tools, small experiments and contingencies;
- no paid advertising in the first phase.

## Financial measures

Keep separate:

1. revenue;
2. contribution after Etsy/payment costs;
3. profit after tools, advertising and operating costs;
4. profit after tax.
