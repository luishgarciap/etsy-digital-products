# Product Ideas and Trial Price Hypotheses

[Home](../README.md) · [Opportunity frame](../discovery/iteration-01/opportunity-frame.md) · [Demand research](../discovery/iteration-01/market-demand.md)

This page preserves the product directions and experimental prices documented in the 10 November 2026 report for Débora. Catholic and colouring directions were added later in the BoK. **None of these ideas or price bands is a final choice.**

## Product families

| Direction | Products considered | Potential angles to investigate |
|---|---|---|
| Cafés / small businesses | Editable café menus, price lists, seasonal signs, loyalty cards, promotions and social post templates | Coordinated kits, straightforward instructions, PT/ES/EN, café and bakery use cases |
| Celebrations | Birthday, baby-shower and wedding invitations; thank-you cards, Christmas/New Year items, signs and coordinated sets | Easy customisation, mobile/print formats, consistent styles and bilingual/cultural collections |
| Children's/family bilingual learning | Flashcards, printable activities, vocabulary games, visual routines and learning resources | Pedagogical/language accuracy, coherent visuals, Portuguese–English or Spanish–English variants |
| Catholic / faith-based | Prayer cards, Rosary, First Communion/Confirmation, children's and bilingual printables | Specific buyers, occasions and originality/licensing |
| Colouring / creative | Printable colouring and creative activity packs | Concrete buyer/use case and content quality, not a vague general category |

New ideas can be added if the research suggests them.

## Original experimental price ranges

| Product | Price hypothesis (USD) | Original reasoning |
|---|---:|---|
| Individual card | $3–5 | Fixed processing fees can reduce contribution |
| Editable invitation | $4–9 | Design and personalisation experience matter |
| Coordinated event bundle | $9–19 | Potential to increase order value |
| Café / small-business kit | $9–25 | More value if it saves business owners time |
| Children's activity/learning bundle | $5–15 | Requires content quality and careful checking |

**These are proposed prices for possible tests, not observed market averages.** Third-party price observations belong in [product economics](../discovery/iteration-01/product-economics.md).

## Timing and practical issues

Seasonal events such as Christmas may create demand, but a new store may have insufficient time to gain visibility. Consider products with year-round use as well. Format, licences, ease of editing, translations and support effort need practical assessment before committing to design.
