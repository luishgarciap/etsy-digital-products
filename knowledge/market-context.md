This page preserves the marketplace-level context of the feasibility report prepared for Débora in November 2026. Company-scale figures describe Etsy as a platform; **they do not establish demand, profitability or expected sales for our products**.

## Marketplace and company

Etsy was established in Brooklyn, New York, in 2005, and operates an international marketplace for creative, personalised, handmade and original digital products. Etsy, Inc. is a US-listed company (NASDAQ: ETSY).

| Measure | Period | Value in original report | Interpretation |

| Active buyers | September 2026 | 87.5 million | Bought at least once in the previous 12 months |

Sources: Etsy Q3 2026 filing [[10]](references.md#ref-10), 2025 annual filing [[26]](references.md#ref-26). Values should be rechecked if used in a new report.

## What the numbers do and do not tell us

- Etsy offers existing buyer traffic, search, international access and automated digital fulfilment.
- The platform is an established business, but discovery and sales are not guaranteed for a new seller.
- The strength of Etsy itself is **not** a market feasibility test for invitations, café kits or flashcards.
- We still need to examine buyer demand, competing offers, prices, reviews, production effort, commissions and actual sales after launch.
- Changes to algorithms, fees, platform policies or demand can affect results.
